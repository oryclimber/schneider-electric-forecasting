# Business term glossary

| Business term | definition |
| --- | --- |
| SE | Schneider Electric |
| Offer margin | Profit margin on a specific product, solution, or deal offered to customers |
| BOM | Bill of Materials |
| ETO | Engineer to Order: Production approach where once a confirmed order for products is received, products are built. |
| RM6 | Specific type of medium-voltage electrical equipment (RM=Ring Main equipment) |
| SF6 | Sulfur Hexafluoride: insulating gas for RM6. |
| RM AirSeT | Schneider Electric's SF6-free Ring Main Unit (RMU). Replaces SF6 with pure air for insulation using a vaccuum. |
| TCO | Total cost of ownership: full, lifetime cost of owning and operating a piece of equipment. |
